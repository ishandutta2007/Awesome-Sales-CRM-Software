# Awesome-Sales-CRM-Software

## Top Sales CRM Software Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Pipeline Management, Contact Tracking & Sales Automation*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial sales CRM platforms** and **open-source projects** that help sales teams manage leads, track deals, automate outreach, and forecast revenue. These tools range from simple contact managers to full revenue intelligence platforms.



**Examples** include Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales, Salesforce Sales Cloud, HubSpot Sales Hub, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive, Freshsales, Insightly, Copper CRM, SugarCRM, and Close CRM (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Sales CRM is a strong open-source domain. **SuiteCRM** leads as the most feature-complete open-source CRM with enterprise-grade capabilities. **EspoCRM** offers a modern, lightweight alternative. **Twenty** brings a modern, Notion-like interface with GraphQL API. **Vtiger** and **Odoo CRM** provide integrated business suites. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Salesforce Sales Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/products/sales-cloud/)**  

  **The enterprise CRM standard** with unmatched ecosystem (AppExchange), customization (Lightning Platform), and AI (Einstein). **The most comprehensive sales platform** — used by 150,000+ companies. Pricing from $25/user/month (Starter) to $500/user/month (Einstein 1 Sales).



- **[Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales](https://dynamics.microsoft.com/en-us/sales/)**  

  Microsoft's sales CRM with deep Office 365, Teams, and Power Platform integration. **The best choice for Microsoft-centric organizations** — Copilot AI included. Pricing from $65/user/month.



- **[HubSpot Sales Hub](https://www.hubspot.com/products/sales)**  

  **The most user-friendly sales CRM** with free tier, email tracking, meeting scheduling, and deal pipelines. **The best starting point for SMBs** — scales from free to enterprise. Pricing from $20/user/month (Starter) to $150/user/month (Enterprise).



- **[Zoho CRM](https://www.zoho.com/crm/)**  

  **The best value CRM** with AI (Zia), omnichannel support, and deep Zoho ecosystem integration. **The most affordable full-featured CRM** — from $14/user/month.



- **[Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com/)**  

  **The most visual sales CRM** — pipeline-focused with drag-and-drop deal stages. **The best for visual pipeline management** — from $14/user/month.



- **[Freshsales](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/sales/)**  

  Freshworks' sales CRM with AI (Freddy), built-in phone, and email. **The best for teams wanting integrated communication** — from $9/user/month.



- **[Insightly](https://www.insightly.com/)**  

  CRM with project management integration. **The best for project-based sales** — from $29/user/month.



- **[Copper CRM](https://www.copper.com/)**  

  **The best CRM for Google Workspace users** — native Gmail, Calendar, and Drive integration. From $12/user/month.



- **[SugarCRM](https://www.sugarcrm.com/)**  

  Enterprise CRM with strong customization and on-premise option. **The most flexible enterprise CRM** — pricing on request.



- **[Close CRM](https://close.com/)**  

  **The best CRM for inside sales** — built-in calling, SMS, and email sequences. From $29/user/month.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  

  **The leading open-source CRM with enterprise-grade features**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **4,500+ GitHub stars** . **Fork of SugarCRM Community Edition** after SugarCRM went commercial . Features **accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities, quotes, invoices, contracts, campaigns, and reports** . **Extensive module ecosystem** — add-ons for marketing automation, quoting, and integrations . **Self-hosted with full data ownership** — no per-user fees, no vendor lock-in . **The de facto open-source Salesforce alternative** — used by organizations of all sizes . **Trade-off**: UI is dated compared to modern CRMs, and setup requires technical expertise . **Best for organizations wanting a full-featured CRM without licensing costs** .



- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)**  

  **Modern, lightweight open-source CRM**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **1,500+ GitHub stars** . **Clean, intuitive interface** — significantly better UX than SuiteCRM . Features **contacts, accounts, leads, opportunities, cases, and campaigns** . **REST API, webhooks, and extensive customization** via Entity Manager . **Self-hosted or cloud** — Docker deployment available . **The best open-source CRM for teams valuing modern UX** . **Trade-off**: Smaller ecosystem than SuiteCRM — fewer third-party modules . **Best for SMBs and teams wanting a modern, maintainable CRM** .



- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  

  **Modern open-source CRM with a Notion-like interface**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **25,000+ GitHub stars** . **The fastest-growing open-source CRM** — modern React/TypeScript/NestJS stack . Features **GraphQL API, custom objects, kanban pipelines, email integration, and workflow automation** . **Self-hosted with Docker** — no per-user fees . **The most modern open-source CRM** — designed for developers and product-led teams . **Trade-off**: Younger than SuiteCRM/EspoCRM — fewer features, rapid development . **Best for startups and tech companies wanting a modern CRM foundation** .



- **[Vtiger CRM Open Source](https://github.com/vtiger-crm/vtigercrm)**  

  **Open-source CRM with sales, marketing, and support automation**, VPL licensed (based on Mozilla Public License) . Features **sales pipeline, email marketing, help desk, inventory, and project management** . **Self-hosted with Docker** . **The most integrated open-source business suite** — includes features beyond sales CRM . **Trade-off**: Complex setup and dated UI . **Best for organizations wanting CRM + support + inventory in one platform** .



- **[Odoo CRM](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  **Open-source CRM module within Odoo ERP**, LGPL-3.0 licensed with **49,000+ GitHub stars** (entire Odoo project) . Features **pipeline management, lead scoring, email integration, and sales automation** . **Part of the comprehensive Odoo suite** — integrates with accounting, inventory, and marketing . **Community edition is free** — enterprise edition adds advanced features . **Best for organizations already using Odoo** or wanting an integrated ERP+CRM .



- **[Dolibarr CRM](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**  

  **Open-source ERP/CRM for small businesses**, GPL-3.0 licensed . Features **contacts, leads, opportunities, proposals, and invoices** . **Simple and quick to deploy** — 1-2 week learning curve . **Best for very small businesses (1-15 employees)** wanting basic CRM + invoicing .



- **[Krayin CRM](https://github.com/krayin/laravel-crm)**  

  **Open-source Laravel CRM for SMEs**, MIT licensed with **8,000+ GitHub stars** . Built with **Laravel 11, Vue.js, and Tailwind CSS** . Features **leads, contacts, accounts, quotes, and activities** . **Modern Laravel stack** — easy for PHP developers to customize . **Best for Laravel developers** wanting a modern CRM foundation .



- **[Monica](https://github.com/monicahq/monica)**  

  **Open-source personal CRM** for managing personal relationships, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **20,000+ GitHub stars** . **Not a sales CRM** — designed for personal relationship management . Features **contacts, reminders, activities, and notes** . **Best for individuals managing personal networks** — not suitable for sales teams .



- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)**  

  **Open-source CRM for nonprofits and associations**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . Features **donor management, memberships, events, and contributions** . **Not a sales CRM** — designed for nonprofit workflows . **Best for nonprofits and associations** needing constituent management .



- **[Odoo CRM (Community)](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)** — Already listed above. **The most comprehensive open-source business suite with CRM** .

- **YetiForce CRM** — Open-source CRM based on Vtiger with modern UI, GPL-3.0 licensed .

- **SuiteCRM 8** — Modernized SuiteCRM with Angular frontend and improved UX .

- **X2CRM** — Open-source CRM with marketing automation and sales force automation .

- **Zurmo** — Gamified open-source CRM (development stalled).

- **Fat Free CRM** — Lightweight Ruby on Rails CRM, MIT licensed .

- **EspoCRM** — Already listed. **Modern and lightweight** .

- **Twenty** — Already listed. **The most modern open-source CRM** .



**Frameworks for building custom sales CRM solutions**: Choose based on team size and requirements. **SuiteCRM** for the most feature-complete open-source CRM with enterprise-grade capabilities . **EspoCRM** for modern UX with lower complexity . **Twenty** for startups and tech companies wanting a modern, API-first CRM foundation . **Odoo CRM** for organizations already using Odoo ERP . **Krayin CRM** for Laravel developers wanting a modern PHP-based CRM . **Vtiger** for CRM + help desk + inventory in one platform . Note that true enterprise CRM with AI-driven forecasting, deep marketing automation, and global ecosystem integrations remains primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong contact, pipeline, and sales automation foundations that require integration for complete revenue operations.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- CRM platforms handle sensitive customer data including contact information, purchase history, and communication records. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and compliance with data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA).

- **Open-source CRM requires operational responsibility** — hosting, security patching, backups, and upgrades are your responsibility. SuiteCRM and EspoCRM have active communities; Twenty is newer with rapid development .

- **UI maturity varies significantly** — SuiteCRM and Vtiger have dated interfaces; EspoCRM and Twenty prioritize modern UX . Evaluate against your team's expectations .

- **Data migration from commercial CRM is non-trivial** — plan for field mapping, relationship preservation, and workflow recreation. Budget for migration services if needed.

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong contact, pipeline, and sales automation foundations, but **AI-driven forecasting, deep marketing automation, and global ecosystem integrations** remain primarily commercial offerings.
